Born from a vision to transform India's co-curricular landscape

Rhetorix was conceived to provide a dedicated platform where individuals can cultivate and showcase their public speaking, debating, and cultural co-curricular skills—areas often overshadowed by the predominant focus on sports in India. The initiative seeks to address this imbalance by fostering environments that encourage articulate expression, critical thinking, and leadership through verbal and cultural engagement.

Rhetorix's core mission is to transform the co-curricular landscape by creating structured opportunities for students to explore and develop these essential skills. By organizing debates, eloquence contests, and cultural forums, the platform aspires to nurture a generation capable of constructive dialogue and societal contribution through effective communication.

"The power of speech and cultural participation are indispensable tools for individual and collective advancement."

"Words and Wisdom — the only two things you need to be successful."

Rhetorix was never created to be a business. It was created to answer a feeling I could never ignore — that words, when backed by intellect, are the most powerful force a person can possess. Stronger than position. Stronger than privilege. Strong enough to shape reality itself.

I often found myself questioning the world around me.

Why do we admire skill, but fail to build it in others?
Why does the youth have potential, but not always direction?
Why is articulation misunderstood as talent, and not recognised as discipline?
Why is oratorship treated as something casual, when it holds the power to influence nations — yet it is not respected like sports, where effort is visible and honoured?

Rhetorix became my answer to those questions.

Not a perfect answer. But an honest one.

I did not build Rhetorix because I had everything figured out. I built it to figure things out. To create a platform where rhetoric, culture, literature, and intellect are not just celebrated, but respected. A platform where the youth does not just participate, but evolves.

Somewhere along this journey, what started as a question became a responsibility. And God has been generous enough to recognise this effort and guide me forward.

Rhetorix is not mine alone.
It belongs to every young mind that refuses to stay silent.
It belongs to those who dare to think differently, speak responsibly, and stand fearlessly.

This is for the youth. From the youth. By the youth.

Be respectful.
Be intelligent.
Be fearless.

Because the future will not be shaped by those who stay silent — but by those who have the courage to speak, and the wisdom to lead.

Committees - Prastavana Conclave 2.0

Explore the diverse committees and their detailed agendas for our upcoming conference.

Lok Sabha

📜 Committee Brief:

The Lok Sabha, or House of the People, is the lower house of India's Parliament. Members are directly elected by the people of India and represent the voice of the nation. This committee simulates the intense debates and legislative processes of India's premier legislative body.

🎯 Agenda:

Discussion of reforming the reservation system in India by shifting towards an economically-based concession framework

📋 Key Discussion Points:

  • Examine structural limitations of caste-based reservations and their effectiveness in addressing present-day socio-economic inequality
  • Evaluate income, wealth, and deprivation indicators as alternative or supplementary bases for affirmative action
  • Analyze potential impacts on social justice, political stability, and constitutional safeguards
  • Propose pragmatic, legally viable strategies for reform while ensuring protection of genuinely marginalized communities

All India Political Party Meet (AIPPM)

📜 Committee Brief:

AIPPM (All India Political Parties Meet) is a high-stakes simulation where delegates represent Indian political parties to debate pressing national issues, balance ideology with pragmatism, and negotiate policy outcomes within the framework of India's democratic system.

🎯 Agenda:

Deliberation on a bill to mandate minimum educational qualifications (up to Master's level) for elected representatives to ensure effective governance and national development

📋 Key Discussion Points:

  • Discuss the need for educational qualifications for elected representatives
  • Examine the constitutional and democratic implications of such a mandate
  • Analyze its impact on inclusivity, representation, and social equity
  • Explore possible alternatives or safeguards to ensure effective governance without exclusion

UN Security Council (UNSC)

📜 Committee Brief:

The United Nations Security Council is charged with maintaining international peace and security. With 15 members (5 permanent with veto power and 10 non-permanent), it's the UN's most powerful body, capable of authorizing military action, sanctions, and peacekeeping operations.

🎯 Agenda:

Deliberation on a bill to mandate minimum educational qualifications (up to Master's Level) for elected representatives to ensure effective governance and national development

📋 Key Discussion Points:

  • Discuss the necessity of minimum educational qualifications for elected representatives
  • Examine constitutional validity and democratic principles involved
  • Analyze the impact on inclusivity, representation, and social equity
  • Explore safeguards or alternative mechanisms to improve governance effectiveness

UN Commission on Status of Women (UNCSW)

📜 Committee Brief:

The UNCSW is the principal global intergovernmental body exclusively dedicated to promoting gender equality and the empowerment of women. It plays a crucial role in monitoring and reviewing progress on gender equality globally.

🎯 Agenda:

Examining the politicization of women's rights: From selective feminism to the exclusion of marginalized genders

📋 Key Discussion Points:

  • Analyze how women's rights are selectively emphasized or ignored for political advantage
  • Examine the exclusion of marginalized genders within mainstream feminist and policy narratives
  • Discuss the social, legal, and cultural consequences of politicizing gender rights
  • Propose inclusive, rights-based approaches that ensure equity across all genders

United Nations International Children's Emergency Fund (UNICEF) For Classes 5-8

📜 Committee Brief:

UNICEF (United Nations Children's Fund) works to protect the rights and well-being of children worldwide by providing humanitarian aid, promoting education and healthcare, and advocating for child protection and development.

🎯 Agenda:

Protecting young children from Internet access special focus on Artificial Intelligence and Social Media

📋 Key Discussion Points:

  • Examine the risks posed by artificial intelligence and social media exposure to young children
  • Discuss the role of governments, platforms, and parents in regulating and monitoring access
  • Analyze the impact of early internet exposure on child development, privacy, and mental health
  • Propose safeguards, policies, and technological solutions to ensure child safety online

International Press (IP)

📜 Committee Brief:

The International Press committee acts as journalists covering the proceedings of other committees. Members write articles, conduct interviews, take photographs, and report on debate outcomes, providing comprehensive media coverage of the entire conference.

🎯 Responsibilities:

  • Daily newspaper publication covering all committee proceedings
  • Conducting interviews with delegates, executive board members, and organizers
  • Photography and videography of key moments
  • Writing feature articles, opinion pieces, and news reports

Indian Premier League

📜 Committee Brief:

The IPL Committee offers a fast paced and strategic simulation based on team management and negotiations. Delegate participate in auctions, decision-making and competitive planning

Note:

  • Only a one day event
  • Can participate in teams of 4 or individually

How is the fee calculated?
The fee is based on your FIRST preferred committee. If you select IPL as your first choice, you can register as an individual (₹900) or as a team (₹3500 for 4 people).
